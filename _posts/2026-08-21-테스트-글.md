flowchart TD
    START([entry]) --> start_node

    start_node -->|alarm_entry| load_alarm_context
    start_node -->|general| resolve_pending_clarification

    resolve_pending_clarification -->|propose_remote_action| propose_remote_action
    resolve_pending_clarification -->|generate_answer| editor_debug
    resolve_pending_clarification -->|continue| resolve_pending_remote_action_response

    resolve_pending_remote_action_response -->|classify_query| classify_query
    resolve_pending_remote_action_response -->|resolve_remote_action| resolve_remote_action
    resolve_pending_remote_action_response -->|prepare_jira_content| prepare_jira_content
    resolve_pending_remote_action_response -->|studio_agent| studio_agent
    resolve_pending_remote_action_response -->|end_gate| end_gate

    classify_query -->|app_help / non_incident<br/>ops_knowledge / uncertain| editor_debug
    classify_query -->|ops_task| resolve_turn_context

    resolve_turn_context --> load_alarm_context
    load_alarm_context -->|fail: error/final_answer| editor_debug
    load_alarm_context -->|ok| refresh_turn_context

    refresh_turn_context -->|plan: 메일 진입| plan_datasources
    refresh_turn_context -->|gate: reload 경로| resolved_target_gate
    refresh_turn_context -->|refine| refine_query
    refine_query --> resolved_target_gate

    resolved_target_gate -->|clarify| editor_debug
    resolved_target_gate -->|reload / refresh_resource| load_alarm_context
    resolved_target_gate -->|ok| plan_datasources

    plan_datasources -->|generate_answer| editor_debug
    plan_datasources -->|propose_remote_action| propose_remote_action
    plan_datasources -->|dispatch_datasources| plan_execution_stages

    plan_execution_stages --> dispatch_current_stage
    dispatch_current_stage -.->|Send 병렬| ttq_pipeline
    dispatch_current_stage -.->|Send 병렬| es_pipeline
    dispatch_current_stage -.->|Send 병렬| druid_pipeline
    dispatch_current_stage -.->|Send 병렬| guideline_pipeline
    dispatch_current_stage -.->|Send 병렬| remote_exec_pipeline
    dispatch_current_stage -->|대상 없음| merge_results

    ttq_pipeline --> merge_stage_results
    es_pipeline --> merge_stage_results
    druid_pipeline --> merge_stage_results
    guideline_pipeline --> merge_stage_results
    remote_exec_pipeline --> merge_stage_results

    merge_stage_results -->|남은 stage| dispatch_current_stage
    merge_stage_results -->|완료| merge_results
    merge_results --> editor_debug

    editor_debug -->|generate_answer| generate_answer
    editor_debug -->|terminal 종료| ENDN([END])

    generate_answer -->|propose_remote_action| propose_remote_action
    generate_answer -->|has_actions| prepare_jira_content
    generate_answer -->|studio_agent| studio_agent
    generate_answer -->|end_gate| end_gate

    propose_remote_action -->|pending 생성| end_gate
    propose_remote_action -->|남은 복합 액션| prepare_jira_content
    resolve_remote_action --> end_gate

    prepare_jira_content --> execute_jira_action
    execute_jira_action -->|studio_agent| studio_agent
    execute_jira_action -->|end_gate| end_gate

    studio_agent -->|generation| studio_url_start
    studio_agent -->|operation| studio_param_start
    studio_agent -->|done| end_gate
    studio_url_start --> studio_url_agent --> studio_url_end --> end_gate
    studio_param_start --> studio_param_agent --> studio_param_end --> end_gate

    end_gate -->|close_editor| mark_terminal_editor_close
    end_gate -->|editor 종료됨| ENDN
    mark_terminal_editor_close --> editor_debug
